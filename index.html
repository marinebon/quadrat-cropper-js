Welcome 👋

This app finds the PVC quadrat frame (mint-green or yellow) in each photo and crops the square opening (25 × 25 cm) automatically. Low-confidence crops are flagged so you can drag the corner markers yourself.

It is built for the MBON Pole to Pole network's standardized rocky-shore photo-quadrat protocol — see marinebon.org/p2p.

Getting images in — any one of:

  1. Drag & drop image files onto the page.
  2. Paste a Drive link to a single photo (shared as “Anyone with the link”) — works instantly, no sign-in.
  3. Bulk from Drive: either right-click the folder in Drive → Download and drop the .zip here (no sign-in), or do a one-time Google sign-in (⚙ button) and paste the folder link.

Optionally drop in the trip .csv files — their columns get merged into the export spreadsheet.

How to use

  1. Add images: drop files / a Drive-exported .zip, or paste a Drive link. Single-photo links work with no setup; whole folders need the .zip trick or a one-time sign-in (⚙).
  2. Wait for the scan: green ring = confident auto-crop (confidence ≥ 0.80), amber = review suggested (0.55–0.80), red = needs manual corners (< 0.55 or detection failed), blue = you cropped it by hand in the editor. The suggested crop is always a square inside the photo; if the detected outline was not square, ran off the photo or is much smaller/larger than the rest of the batch, it is flagged amber/red for review.
  3. Review: click any thumbnail to open the full photo with the crop's corner markers and a live crop preview alongside. Drag the markers onto the inner corners of the frame, then Apply & re-crop (failed detections open the same page so you can place the markers by hand).
  4. Export ZIP — no ticking needed: every crop with ≥ 80% confidence is included automatically, plus any you adjusted manually (adjusting a red/amber one always includes it). Images without a usable frame are skipped but listed in manifest.csv so nothing is silently lost.

Best results follow the P2P protocol as-designed: the camera 42 cm above the surface, whole quadrat inside the photo, the frame shaded for even lighting, and no tide pools in shot. Pre-cropped images (no green frame) are rejected rather than guessed.

These photo-quadrats follow the MBON Pole to Pole protocol

Your crops feed directly into the network's standardized rocky-shores monitoring: a 20 mm PVC frame with an OM System Tough TG-7 camera mounted 42 cm above the surface photographs a 25 × 25 cm opening; 40–60 photo-quadrats are taken per intertidal level (3 levels per site, 3 sites > 1 km apart per locality).

Protocol & network: marinebon.org/p2p — field manual (EN) · manual de campo (ES) · DOI 10.25607/OBP-2014.3.

This tool is open source — view the code on GitHub or report an image that fails (wrong crop, missed frame, crash… the more detail the better). The detector is being actively improved with real-world shots like yours.

Crop

Both keep the yellow/green PVC rim out of the crop. A fixed size (camera at a fixed height) gives every photo the same square, centred and rotated on the detected frame. Saving a change re-crops the photos already processed, except those adjusted by hand.

This site already provides a key: leave the field empty to use it.

Adds high-resolution Esri World Imagery to the Map tab. Free key from an ArcGIS Location Platform account (privilege: basemaps). Without it the map uses Sentinel-2 cloudless (10 m) and OpenStreetMap.

Every crop shows the same 25 × 25 cm, so a common size in px means a common scale (px per cm) — crops become directly comparable. The px box is used with "fixed size".

Manual corner review

Drag the four corner markers onto the inner corners of the PVC opening. The crop area updates live.

Keys: R reset to auto-detected, Enter apply & re-crop, Esc close, ← / → previous / next photo (moved markers are applied before moving on).

Crop preview
Detector: pure-JS HSV mask → ray-casting → sequential RANSAC → per-edge refinement. Auto-accept ≥ 0.80 · review < 0.80 · manual < 0.55.
